# Tableau-Project


I completed two Tableau projects focused on analysing real-world datasets and presenting insights through interactive dashboards. These exercises helped me develop practical skills in data visualisation, trend analysis, dashboard design, and data storytelling. 

## 🌍 Task 1: Global Health Analysis

Using the **Gapminder Health** dataset, I created a series of visualisations to explore global health trends and disparities across countries and continents. The project included:

- Life Expectancy by Continent (Bar Chart)
- Life Expectancy Trend Over Time (Line Chart)
- Population Distribution by Gender (Pie Chart)
- Life Expectancy vs BMI (Scatter Plot)
- Additional visualisation of my choice
- Interactive dashboard creation

### Key Reflection

As life expectancy continues to rise, healthcare organisations such as the NHS may need to prepare for an ageing population by investing in elderly care services, long-term support, and chronic disease management. 

## 🎵 Task 2: Spotify Data Analysis

For the second project, I analysed a Spotify dataset containing information about music genres, popularity scores, and audio characteristics such as danceability, energy, tempo, and valence.

The goal was to identify trends and relationships within the music industry through Tableau visualisations and dashboard design.

### Key Findings

- Songs with higher popularity often had higher danceability scores.
- High danceability alone did not guarantee a song would be popular.
- **Pop** was identified as the most popular genre within the dataset.
